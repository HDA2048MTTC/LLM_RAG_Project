Simple Daily Habits to Increase Fiber
This document focuses on easy ways to eat more fiber, including oatmeal with berries, chia seeds, nuts, and high-fiber breakfasts.

Smart Food Swaps
Examples include switching white bread, rice, or pasta for whole-grain versions.

High-Fiber Snacks
Suggestions include fruits, vegetables, nuts, seeds, popcorn, and whole-grain crackers.

Add Beans Often and Check Food Labels
Beans and lentils can be added to many meals. When buying packaged foods, look for items containing 3–5 grams of fiber per serving.

Hydration
Drinking enough water supports digestive comfort, as fiber absorbs water.
