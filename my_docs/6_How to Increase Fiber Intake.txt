How Much Fiber You Need Daily
This document answers the common question: How much fiber should adults eat? Most adults need 25–38 grams per day depending on age and sex.

Practical Ways to Add Fiber
Suggestions include replacing refined grains with whole grains, adding fruits and vegetables to meals, and incorporating fiber-rich foods like beans, lentils, nuts, and seeds.

Staying Hydrated
Because fiber absorbs water, sufficient hydration is essential to prevent discomfort such as bloating.

Increase Fiber Gradually
A gradual increase allows the digestive system to adjust and improves comfort.
