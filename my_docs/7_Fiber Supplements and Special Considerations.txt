When Fiber Supplements Are Helpful
This document explains when people might use fiber supplements like psyllium to support digestion, cholesterol, and blood sugar control.

Benefits of Psyllium
Psyllium forms a thick gel when mixed with water, softens stool, and promotes regularity. It is gentle on the digestive system and less likely to cause gas.

IBS and Digestive Sensitivity
People with IBS may react to fast-fermenting fibers (beans, onions, wheat). Switching to slower-fermenting fibers like psyllium or oats can reduce discomfort.

Medication Interactions and Whole Foods
Supplements should be spaced away from medications. Whole foods still provide nutrients that supplements cannot replace.
