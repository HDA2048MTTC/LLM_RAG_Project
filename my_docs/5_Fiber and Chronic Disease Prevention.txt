Overview: Fiber and Disease Risk Reduction
This document explains how fiber intake supports long-term health, including heart disease, diabetes, and colon cancer prevention.

Heart Health Benefits
Soluble fiber binds to bile acids, causing the body to use cholesterol to replace them. This lowers LDL (“bad”) cholesterol and supports heart health.

Blood Sugar Control
Fiber slows the entry of sugar into the bloodstream and helps maintain stable blood glucose levels. Whole grains and legumes rich in fiber are linked to lower risk of type 2 diabetes.

Colon Cancer Prevention
Fiber increases stool bulk and dilutes harmful substances in the colon. Fermentable fibers create short-chain fatty acids that support colon cell health.

Weight Management and Overall Impact
Fiber increases fullness, regulates appetite, and contributes to lower long-term risks of chronic diseases.
